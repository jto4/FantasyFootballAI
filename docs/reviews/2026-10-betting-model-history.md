# Betting game-model history — October 2026

This replaces eighteen dated per-version reviews (v2–v18, October 12–13). Each version tried to
beat the sportsbook's own no-vig probability for NFL moneyline, spread and total. **None did
consistently.** Every pooled book-improvement interval included zero, and no paper selection ever
passed the app's action gates. Production stayed on `market-context-v3` (moneyline, spread) and
`health-weather-total-v4` (totals); v5–v18 were research only. New versions are paused (see
[decisions](../decisions.md), "Price checking over new betting models"), and the
[October 22 test](2026-10-22-betting-model-test.md) is the current measurement.

Shared method: parameters chosen on older seasons (usually 2016–2021), then scored on 3,221 matched
non-push 2022–2025 outcomes at each game's own archived line and paired prices, plus a small 2026
sample. Those later seasons were reused across versions, so they are retrospective evidence, not an
untouched holdout. Brier/log loss measure probability accuracy, not executable betting returns.
Research data lives in [`docs/research/`](../research/); the scripts are in `scripts/research/`.

Reference Brier on the 2022–2025 archive: book 0.236666, v3 0.236606, v4 production 0.236587.

Lessons kept in [`memory.md`](../memory.md) and [`decisions.md`](../decisions.md): select on older
seasons before looking at later ones, never call a reused sample a holdout, keep undated injury or
weather data out of historical features, and treat the sportsbook as an explicit input rather than
claiming independent prediction.
